BlockFair privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026. Applies to the BlockFair app for Android, iOS and the web.

The short version

BlockFair has no accounts and no server of its own. Your games, scores and settings stay on your device. The app is free and funded by ads; the ad service and the crash and usage reporting it uses collect a small amount of technical data, described below. You can decline personalised ads, and in some regions you are asked first.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, the app keeps these on your device only: your saved games, scores, statistics, streaks, achievements and unlocked palettes; your settings (sound, theme, language, layout, reminder); the results of daily and weekly boards and a short replay of your last moves. None of this is sent to us. Clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app, removes all of it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Android and iOS apps show ads through Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. Ads appear only at breaks: after a game ends, on the pause screen, when you open the app again, and when you choose to watch one to continue a run. Never during play.

To request and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK collects and shares with Google: your IP address (which indicates an approximate location), device information and identifiers (the Android advertising ID and app set ID, or the iOS identifier for advertisers when you allow tracking), how you interact with the app and with ads (launches, taps, video views) and diagnostic data about the SDK's performance. Google uses this to serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud; depending on your choices it may also personalise ads. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and in US states with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent or offers you choices before the first ad, through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Help → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in the system settings (Settings → Google → Ads); on iOS, under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Without personalisation you still see ads, but they are not based on your interests.

Crash and usage reporting (Firebase)

The Android and iOS apps use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, both provided by Google LLC, so that we can see whether the game works and which parts people use. They collect: app interactions (such as which modes are opened, when a game ends and whether an ad was shown), app and device information (model, operating system version, language, app version), a randomly generated installation identifier, crash reports with stack traces and the state of the app at the time of a crash, and diagnostic data. We do not send any name, e-mail address, account identifier or anything you typed. This data is encrypted in transit and is kept by Google on our behalf under its standard retention settings (usage data for up to 14 months, crash data for 90 days). Google's practices are described at Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Ad configuration file

At start the app may download a small settings file from our website that says which ad formats are on and how often they may appear. The request carries your IP address like any web request; nothing about you is sent or stored.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, e-mail address, phone number or payment information. The app sells nothing.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites by us. Ad personalisation, where you allow it, is Google's and is governed by the choices above.

Children

BlockFair is made for a general audience of players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are requested with a content rating of PG or stricter.

Sharing with third parties

The only recipients of data are Google (AdMob, Firebase), as described above, for the purposes described above. The daily result grids and seed codes you share leave the app only when you tap Share and only to the app you choose.

Deleting your data

Data on your device: clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data held by Google: reset your advertising ID (Android) or disable tracking (iOS) to unlink future ad data, and use the Ad privacy choices in the app to withdraw consent. To ask us to delete the analytics and crash data tied to your installation, write to the address below with the approximate date you installed the app; we will delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If the app ever collects more than described here, this policy changes first and the app's store listing says so.
